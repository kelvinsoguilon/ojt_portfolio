National Telecommunications Commission (NTC)

Republic of the Philippines

About the Agency

The National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) is the government agency responsible for the supervision, adjudication, and control over all telecommunications services, radio, and television networks throughout the Philippines. Currently an attached agency of the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT), the NTC ensures that the country's public communications infrastructure operates efficiently and compliantly.

Core Mandates

  • Regulation & Supervision: Regulates the installation, operation, and maintenance of radio stations and telecommunications facilities for both private and public use.
  • Spectrum Management: Manages and allocates the radio frequency spectrum to ensure interference-free communication and equitable resource distribution.
  • Consumer Protection: Enforces telecommunications laws and regulations to protect consumer rights, promote fair competition, and ensure reliable service delivery.
